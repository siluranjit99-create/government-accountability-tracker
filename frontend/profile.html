About CivicPulse

Our mission

CivicPulse is dedicated to empowering transparency and unwavering accountability in public infrastructure. We believe in providing a clear, objective platform for citizens to report, track, and resolve local issues.

Resolution logic

An issue is marked solved once it reaches a confirmation threshold from other users, not just the original poster. Our data-driven approach ensures critical infrastructure needs are highlighted clearly to local authorities.
